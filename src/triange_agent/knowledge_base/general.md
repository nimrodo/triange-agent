# General Support Policy

This document covers support scope, response expectations, and handling for
requests that don't fall cleanly into billing, technical, or account
categories.

## Support Scope

General inquiries include feature requests, product feedback, questions
about plan differences that aren't tied to an active billing action, and
requests for guidance on how to use a feature that is working as intended
(as opposed to a technical fault). Feature requests are logged to the
product feedback system but are not tracked or promised a resolution
timeline to the customer — support should thank the customer and log the
request rather than committing to any delivery date, since prioritization is
outside support's control.

## Response Time Expectations

Standard-tier customers can expect a first response within 24 business
hours; priority-tier customers (identified by their plan or by an active
enterprise contract) receive a first response within 4 business hours.
These targets apply to first response only, not full resolution — a
complex issue may require multiple exchanges and updates beyond the first
response window. If an issue cannot be resolved within 5 business days of
the first response, the assigned agent should proactively send a status
update to the customer even if there is nothing new to report, rather than
letting the ticket go quiet.

## Language and Accessibility Requests

Support is provided in English by default, with translated responses
available on request through the translation tool for a fixed set of
supported languages. Accessibility-related requests — such as a customer
asking for documentation in a screen-reader-friendly format, or reporting
that a page fails to work with assistive technology — are treated as a
distinct category from general feature feedback and are escalated to the
accessibility review queue rather than the general product feedback system,
since these carry compliance implications feature requests do not.

## Abuse and Policy Violations

Reports that another user is violating platform policy (harassment, spam,
prohibited content) are not resolved by first-line support directly; they
are escalated to the trust-and-safety queue with the reporting customer's
account ID and any evidence provided. First-line support should acknowledge
the report and set expectations that trust-and-safety will follow up
directly, rather than promising a specific outcome or timeline, since the
investigation may take longer than standard response targets.

## Duplicate and Multi-Channel Tickets

When a customer submits the same request through multiple channels (for
example, both a support ticket and a social media message), the tickets
should be merged under the earliest-opened ticket, and the customer should
be told which channel to expect a response on so redundant replies aren't
sent from multiple channels simultaneously. Merging duplicate tickets does
not reset the response-time clock — the original ticket's creation time
still governs the response time expectations described above.

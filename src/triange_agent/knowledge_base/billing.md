# Billing Policy

This document covers how charges, refunds, disputes, and subscription billing
are handled for customer accounts.

## Refunds

Refund requests are accepted within 30 days of the original charge date. To
qualify, the purchase must not have already been fulfilled in a way that
consumes the majority of its value (for example, a downloaded digital good
that has already been fully accessed). Approved refunds are processed back to
the original payment method within 5–7 business days. If the original payment
method has been closed or expired, the refund is issued as account credit
instead, and the customer is notified by email when the credit is applied.

Partial refunds are available for mid-cycle subscription cancellations,
prorated to the number of unused days remaining in the billing period. Partial
refunds are not available for one-time purchases; those are either refunded in
full or not at all, based on the 30-day window above.

## Double Charges

A double charge occurs when the same order or subscription renewal is billed
twice within a 24-hour window due to a payment processor retry or a duplicate
submission. Double charges are auto-detected by the billing system within one
business day and refunded automatically without the customer needing to file
a request. If a customer reports a suspected double charge before the
automatic detection has run, the support agent should confirm both charges
share the same order ID or subscription ID before manually issuing the
refund — charges for two genuinely separate orders placed close together are
not double charges and should not be refunded under this policy.

## Disputes and Chargebacks

If a customer initiates a chargeback with their bank rather than going through
support, the account is placed on billing hold until the dispute resolves.
While on hold, the customer retains read access to their account but cannot
make new purchases or renew subscriptions. Support agents cannot lift a
billing hold directly; it is lifted automatically once the payment processor
reports the chargeback outcome. Customers should always be encouraged to
contact support before filing a chargeback, since chargebacks take
significantly longer to resolve (often 45–90 days) than a direct refund
request.

## Proration

When a customer upgrades their subscription plan mid-cycle, the new plan's
cost is prorated for the remaining days in the current billing period, and the
prorated amount is charged immediately. When a customer downgrades mid-cycle,
no immediate charge or refund occurs — the lower price takes effect at the
start of the next billing cycle, and the customer keeps their current plan's
features until then. Annual plans prorate the same way as monthly plans, just
against the annual period instead of the monthly one.

## Failed Payments

When a subscription renewal payment fails, the system retries the charge
three times over seven days before suspending the subscription. Customers
are emailed after each failed attempt. A suspended subscription can be
reactivated by updating the payment method and confirming a manual charge;
no additional penalty fee is applied for a failed-then-recovered payment.
